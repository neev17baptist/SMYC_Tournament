Message to All Parishes & Youth

Dear Respected Fathers and Youth Animators,

We, the Indian Catholic Youth Movement (ICYM) unit of St. Mary's Church, are immensely joyful to announce our first annual inter-parish football tournament, VERTEX 2026.

Sport has a unique power to unite communities, build character, and foster deep fellowship among our youth. This tournament is designed not only to showcase athletic talent on the pitch but to strengthen bonds across parishes within the Archdiocese of Kolkata. I warmly invite all parishes to send in your teams and partake in this celebration of youth and sportsmanship.

"May God bless your preparations and guide every player on and off the field."

Official Guidelines

Complete Tournament Rules

All 22 regulations governing VERTEX 2026.

01

Each member participating should be a Christian and carry a valid photo ID proof. Any team found with a non-Catholic participant will be disqualified without a refund.

02

An additional team manager who will take responsibility for the team. He/she will be the ONLY one to bring any concerns to the table and the name of that person should be mentioned in the registration form. If any player approaches the table, it will disqualify the team from the next match.

03

Each team should have 4 players on field and 2 players as substitutes.

04

All teams should be in proper uniform. Each team should have the same colour jerseys, properly numbered.

05

The referee's decision remains final.

06

There will be no Offside.

07

Indoor football shoes or Keds are compulsory for every player.

08

Corners to be taken from the side.

09

If the opponents enter the "D" the goal will be disallowed.

10

During a free kick, corners and throw in there should be a minimum distance of 3 steps between the ball and the opponent.

11

If the ball hits the ceiling, fans or lights, it will be called a high ball.

12

Sliding tackles are not allowed.

13

Throws-ins to be taken from outside the line.

14

Direct goals from throw-ins are not valid (must deflect).

15

Entering own D by mistake - penalty.

16

Penalties should be taken post to post.

17

If a player gets a red card (or two yellow cards) in one match or two yellow cards in two consecutive matches, then he will miss the following match.

18

Total points acquired and goal difference matters at the time of seeing who qualifies from each group.

19

Each win assures 3 (three) points to the winner, and a draw assures 1 (one) point to both the teams.

20

If any team is late to report for their match (Teams get 1 minute to report to the field), then the opposition wins based on walkover and wins by 3 points awarded with 3 goals scored.

21

Under all given circumstances, the organizers can intervene and the organizers' decision is final.

22

Every entry for the non-participants is only Rs. 20/-.
